# The feel check

Run it after the harness is clean, against the contact sheets and a live scroll. The harness measures whether the page works; this measures whether it does what it was for.

Do it in this order, and do not reread `docs/scroll-plan.md` first. The whole value is in arriving cold.

1. **Scroll the page top to bottom at a normal reading pace.** Once. No stopping to fix things.
2. **Write one word per act for what you felt**, before looking at anything else. If an act produces no word, write nothing for it; nothing is the finding.
3. **Open the plan and diff the two curves.**

Where they disagree, the page is wrong, not the plan. Rewriting the intended curve to match what got built turns the plan into a description of the accident.

Then three checks:

- **Does the peak read as the peak?** On the sheet it should be the largest visual change and hold the most scroll room. If a different act is the biggest thing on the sheet, that act is the real peak and the plan lost: fix the page, or admit the new peak in the plan and give it the budget.
- **Is there silence in front of it?** If the act before the peak is as loud as the peak, the peak has nothing to arrive from.
- **Does the end resolve?** The last screen can stand still with content on it. A blank final frame, a cue that faded out or a footer that just begins means the page ended rather than finished.

Two adjacent acts that produced the same word in step 2 is the filler finding caught late; cutting one is almost always right.

Report the intended curve, the felt curve and what you changed. A build that reports them identical on the first pass either got lucky or did not do the check cold.
